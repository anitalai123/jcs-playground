Questions you're likely to be asked

Practise answering out loud or write your answer here. For questions about your experience, use the STAR method: describe the situation, the task, the action you took and the result.

What they're looking for

From the job advert, wants someone who:

  • explains things clearly and politely, on the phone and face to face
  • stays calm when people are upset or when it's busy
  • is comfortable using computer systems to update records
  • works well as part of a team

Think of one example from your experience for each of these. Examples can come from work, volunteering, caring or everyday life.

Find out before the interview

  • what does and who it helps
  • its values, usually on the "About us" page of its website
  • any recent news about it

Questions you could ask them

  • What does a typical day look like in this role?
  • What training would I get when I start?
  • What do people who do well in this team have in common?
