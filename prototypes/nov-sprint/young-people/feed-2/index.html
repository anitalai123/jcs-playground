Get paid work experience if you’re on Universal Credit

  • Jobs guaranteeA paid job for 6 months if you’re 16 to 24 and have been on benefits for more than 1 year.
  • Sector-based work academy programme (SWAP)6 weeks of pre-employment training, work experience and a guaranteed job interview.
  • Work coachGet support from a work coach to find opportunities and help you into work.
