Finding a job is a job in itself

Set specific working hours (for example, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM) so you do not feel like you are "on call" looking for work 24 hours a day.

Work experience if you’re on Universal Credit

  • Jobs Guarantee6-month paid job placement if you’re 16 to 24 and have been on benefits for more than 1 year.
  • Sector-based work academy placements (SWAPs)6-week work experience placement and a guaranteed job interview.
  • Work coachIf you’re on UC, you’ll get support from a work coach to help you back into work.

Get support in work

Check if you can get extra help such as workplace adjustments if you have a disability and knowing your rights at work, and tips for progressing your career. You may also have questions about how a new job affects your benefits.

Do not go it alone – speak to a careers adviser

A careers adviser can help you:

  • take the first step in your career
  • work out what jobs you might enjoy
  • change jobs or progress in your current career
  • decide on an ideal training choice
  • Speak to a DWP careers adviserFor 18 and above
  • Speak to your school careers adviserFor under 18s

Things feeling too much?

Looking for work can be stressful and you’re not alone. Taking one step at a time can help break down a job search. Taking care of your mental health along the way is important when times get hard.
