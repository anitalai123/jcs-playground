Work experience if you’re on Universal Credit

Jobs Guarantee6-month paid job placement if you’re 16 to 24 and have been on benefits for more than 1 year.
Sector-based work academy placements (SWAPs)6-week work experience placement and a guaranteed job interview.
Work coachIf you’re on UC, you’ll get support from a work coach to help you back into work.

Get support in work

Things feeling too much?

Looking for work can be stressful and you’re not alone. Taking one step at a time can help break down a job search. Taking care of your mental health along the way is important when times get hard.
